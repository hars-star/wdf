About StudentHub Portal

StudentHub is an open, accessible, and high-performance academic management platform built for Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT).

Our Core Mission

To eliminate administrative bottlenecks, foster peer collaboration across engineering departments, and empower students to participate in curricular and extracurricular activities seamlessly.

Project Scope & Accreditation

Course Framework

Developed under Web Development Frameworks (ITUE203), 3rd Semester, Faculty of Technology and Engineering.

Accessibility First

Complies with WCAG 2.1 AA standards for keyboard accessibility, ARIA tags, and high-contrast color schemes.
