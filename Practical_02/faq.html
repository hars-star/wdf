Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about StudentHub portal, registration, and events.

1. How do I register for university hackathons and events?

Navigate to the Events page, select the event you wish to participate in, and click "View Details & Register". If you are logged in, your details will be pre-filled automatically.

2. How is my password secured on StudentHub?

We use industry-standard bcrypt hashing with a salt cost factor of 12 via PHP's native password_hash() function. Plain text passwords are never stored.

3. Can I export student records?

Yes, authorized administrators can export the full student roster directly to CSV from the Student Management panel.

4. Who can access the Admin Dashboard?

Only users with the role admin have access to administrative management pages and audit trails. Unauthorized attempts are automatically blocked with role-based checks.
